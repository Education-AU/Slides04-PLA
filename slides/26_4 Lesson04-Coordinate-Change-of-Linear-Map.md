Let's take an example of change of coordinates of a linear map from $\mathbb{R}^2$ to $\mathbb{R}^2$. 

Let $L:\mathbb{R}^2\to \mathbb{R}^2$ be a linear map defined by the matrix
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2\\
3 & 4               
\end{bmatrix}
$$
in the standard basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$.

Now we want to find the matrix representation of the same linear map $L$ in a new basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
Let's choose a new basis for $\mathbb{R}^2$ given by the vectors
$$
\mathbf{e}'_1=\begin{bmatrix}
1\\
1
\end{bmatrix},\quad
\mathbf{e}'_2=\begin{bmatrix}   
1\\
-1
\end{bmatrix}
$$
