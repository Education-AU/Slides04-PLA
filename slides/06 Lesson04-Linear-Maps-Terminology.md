<div class="center">

1. **Domain**:<br> The defining space $V$ is called the domain
2. **Codomain**:<br> The target space $W$ is called the codomain
3. **Image/Range**:<br> $L(V)=Range(L)=Im(L)=\{ L(\mathbf{v}) \mid \mathbf{v} \in V \}\subset W$
4. **Kernel/Null-Space**:<br> $Null(L)=Ker(L)=\{ \mathbf{v} \in V \mid L(\mathbf{v})=\mathbf{0} \}\subset V$
5. **Rank**:<br> $Rank(L)=dim(Range(L))$
