What we want is an expression for the matrix representation of the linear map $L$ in the new bases $\mathbf{e}'_i$
and $\mathbf{f}'_j$ of $V$ and $W$.

That is
$$
d'=A'c'
$$
where $c'$ is the coordinate vector of a vector $v\in V$ in the basis $\mathbf{e}'_i$ and $d'$ is the coordinate vector
of the image $L (v)\in W$ in the basis $\mathbf{f}'_j$.

And where A' is the matrix representation of the linear map $L$ in the new bases $\mathbf{e}'_i$ and $\mathbf{f}'_j$
of $V$ and $W$.

From previous lesson we have studied the change of coordinates in a vector space. We can use that to find the relation
between the coordinates $c$ and $c'$ and between $d$ and $d'$.
