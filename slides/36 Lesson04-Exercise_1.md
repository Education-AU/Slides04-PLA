For each map, determine whether it is linear.

#### Exercise a

Let $T:\mathbb{R}^2\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$ defined by

$ T (x,y)= (2x-y,\ x+3y)$

Is $T$ linear?

#### Exercise b

Let $T:\mathbb{R}^2\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$ defined by

$ T (x,y)= (x+y+1,\ 2x-y)$

Is $T$ linear?

#### Exercise c

Let $T:\mathbb{R}^2\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ defined by

$ T (x,y)=xy$

Is $T$ linear?

Determine the matrix of a linear map

##### Exercise d

Let $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ be defined by

$T (x,y,z)= (x+2y-z,\ 3x+y+2z)$

Find the matrix $A$ such that
$T (\mathbf x)=A\mathbf x$ in the standard basis of $\mathbb R^3$ and $\mathbb R^2$.

Then calculate $T (1,2,-1)$ using matrix multiplication.

#### Exercise e

A linear map $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3$ satisfies
$$
T\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
T\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}3\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Find the matrix of $T$ in standard basis, and calculate $T (2,-1)$.
