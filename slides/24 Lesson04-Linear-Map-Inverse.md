We are now able to conclude an important statements using the **Rank-Nullity theorem**

Consider a linear map 
$$
L:V\rightarrow W
$$

where $V$ and $W$ are finite dimensional vectors spaces

If the map is invertible it must be injective and surjective, that is $\operatorname{dim}(\operatorname{ker}(L))=0$ and $\operatorname{range}(L)=W$ which implies
$$
\operatorname{dim}(W)=\operatorname{dim}(V)
$$
Therefore in order for the map to be invertible the dimensions of domain and codomain must be the same.
