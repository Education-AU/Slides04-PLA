An important property is that, if you combine linear maps through several vectorspaces it is overall a linear map

<div class="h3-blue">
Composition
</div>

Let $U,V,W$ be vectorspaces. And
$$
\begin{aligned}
L:U\to V\\
T:V\to W
\end{aligned}
$$

Then
$$ 
(T\circ L)(\mathbf{v})=T (L (\mathbf{v}))
$$
is a linear map from $U$ to $W$
