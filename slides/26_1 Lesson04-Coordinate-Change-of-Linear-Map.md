Consider a linear map 

$L:V\to W$ between two finite dimensional vectorspaces. 

Let $\{\mathbf{e}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{e}_n\}$ be a basis of $V$ and $\{\mathbf{f}_1,\ldots,\mathbf{f}_m\}$ be a basis of $W$. 

We are assume that the matrix representation of the linear map $L$ in the bases $\mathbf{e}_i$ and $\mathbf{f}_j$ 
of $V$ and $W$ is given.

What we want is to find, is the matrix representation of the same linear map $L$ in the new bases $\mathbf{e}'_i$ and $\mathbf{f}'_j$ of $V$ and $W$.

Now we have been studying coordinate changes so this will be quite easy

The linear map $L$ can be expressed in coordinates as
$$
d=Ac
$$
In the chosen basis $\mathbf{e}_i$ and $\mathbf{f}_j$ of $V$ and $W$ respectively. 

Where $c$ is the coordinate vector of a vector $v\in V$ in the basis $\mathbf{e}_i$ and $d$ is the coordinate vector of the image $L(v)\in W$ in the basis $\mathbf{f}_j$.
