Let $T:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$ be determined by a matrix
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&1\\
2&4&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
in standard basis of $\mathbb R^3$ and $\mathbb R^2$.

Find:
1. Can this map be invertible? Why or why not?
2. $\ker(T)$
3. $\text{im}(T)$
4. $\text{rank}(T)$
5. $\text{nullity}(T)$
6. Is $T$ injective? Is $T$ surjective?
