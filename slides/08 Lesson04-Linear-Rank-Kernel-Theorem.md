An important theorem that we will encounter from time to time is the following

<!-- theorem -->

Consider a linear map $L:V\to W$ where $V$ and $W$ are finite dimensional vectorspaces. Then

$$
\operatorname{dim} (ker (L))+\operatorname{dim} (range (L))=\operatorname{dim} (V)
$$
