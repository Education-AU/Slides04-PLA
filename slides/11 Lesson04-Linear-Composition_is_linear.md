title: Composition of Linear Maps is a Linear Map

<br>

<div class="h3-blue">
Proof:
</div>

$$
(T\circ L)(\mathbf{u_1}+\mathbf{u_2})=T(L(\mathbf{u_1}+\mathbf{u_2}))=
T(L(\mathbf{u_1})+L(\mathbf{u_2}))=
T(L(\mathbf{u_1}))+T(L(\mathbf{u_2}))
=(T\circ L)(\mathbf{u_1})+(T\circ L)(\mathbf{u_2})
$$

$$
(T\circ L)(s\mathbf{u})=T(L(s\mathbf{u}))=
T(sL(\mathbf{u}))=
sT(L(\mathbf{u}))=
s(T\circ L)(\mathbf{u})
$$
