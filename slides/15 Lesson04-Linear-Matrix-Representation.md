Consider **finite dimensional** vectorspaces $V$ dimension $n$ and $W$ dimension $m$ and consider a linear map

$$
T:V \to W
$$
Choose bases in $V$ and $W$

$$
\begin{aligned}
V&:\{\mathbf{e}_1,\mathbf{e}_2,\dots,\mathbf{e}_n \}\\
W&:\{\mathbf{f}_1,\mathbf{f}_2,\dots,\mathbf{f} _m \}
\end{aligned}
$$

Consider $\mathbf{v} \in V$ and expand in the **chosen** basis of $V$

$$
\mathbf{v}=\sum_{j=1}^n c_j\mathbf{e}_j
$$
